A few related mini-courses, each around 6–12 hours, which can be attended separately. A continuation of last term’s topics course, with a focus on open problems. The plan is tentative; topic order and length may change with participants’ interests.

Part I: Representations of Yangians

Yangians and quantum affine algebras: Drinfeld polynomials, tensor products and Nakajima’s geometric construction. We study highest weight categories, category O, and the links with factorisation and quantum toroidal algebras.

Tentative outline
PartTopic
1Yangians and sl2. Evaluation modules, spectral parameters and tensor products.
2Drinfeld polynomials and q-characters. ℓ-weights, the ℓ-root lattice and blocks.
3Highest weight categories. Verma modules, local and global Weyl modules, and BGG reciprocity.
4Nakajima’s construction. Quiver varieties, standard and simple modules, and intersection cohomology.
5Sheaves on configuration spaces. Standard and costandard modules, R-matrices and meromorphic tensor structures.
6Category O. Hernandez–Jimbo, prefundamental modules, Baxter relations and shifted Yangians.
7Toroidal algebras and affine Yangians. Fock modules and connections with W-algebras.

If time permits: open problems about standard modules, multiplicity formulas and factorisation.

Reading: Chari–Pressley, A Guide to Quantum Groups (1994); Molev, Yangians and Classical Lie Algebras (2007); Nakajima, Quiver varieties and finite dimensional representations of quantum affine algebras (2001); Hernandez–Jimbo, Asymptotic representations and Drinfeld rational fractions (2012).
