Research

Cohomological Hall algebras

Cohomological Hall algebras and vertex algebras
Take the moduli stack of objects in a one-dimensional category, e.g. representations of a quiver or coherent sheaves on a curve. It has the structure of a vertex quantum group! The structure maps being the CoHA and Joyce's vertex coproduct.

Orthosymplectic modules of cohomological Hall algebras
Define CoHAs for orthosymplectic gauge groups, with their vertex/CoHAM structure which forms a vertex Yetter-Drinfeld module. Examples: quivers with potential, of preprojective algebras, and of dimension zero sheaves on a smooth proper surface. Give shuffle formulas. Develop a theory of factorisation algebras over "orthosymplectic spacetimes" which these are examples of. Joint with S. DeHority.

Critical CoHAs, vertex coalgebras and Deformed Drinfeld coproducts
We construct a vertex coproduct on the critical cohomological Hall algebra of a quiver with potential and show that it forms a vertex bialgebra. We extend the CoHA by a Cartan part via vertex algebraic bosonisation. For ADE quivers, the vertex coproduct recovers Drinfeld's deformed coproduct on the Yangian. Joint with S. Jindal and S. Kaubrys.


Quantum groups and factorisation algebras

Factorisation quantum groups
What is the structure on A-Mod when A is a Yangian, affine quantum group, ... ? This paper develops the axiomatics of "braided factorisation categories", "factorisation quantum groups", etc., which are analogues of the standard notions but "factorisably over the Ran space". We prove that these structures are controlled by spectral R(z)-matrices, and generalise previous work on q-vertex algebras, deformed chiral algebras and meromorphic tensor categories. This gives a mathematicisation of Costello-Witten-Yamazaki's work on 4d Chern-Simons theory.


Algebraic geometry
Virtual localization revisited and The stacky concentration theorem
We generalise Atiyah-Bott's localisation formula so that it is powerful enough to deal e.g. with moduli stacks: instead of manifolds, we consider ~arbitrary Artin stacks, and instead of localising along torus-equivariant classes, we localise along Chern classes of line bundles. In this setting, we show when the localised cohomology is concentrated on a closed substack, and give a generalisation of Graber-Pandharipande's formula for the virtual fundamental class in terms of the class of the closed substack. Joint with D. Aranha, A. Khan, H. Park, and C. Ravi.
