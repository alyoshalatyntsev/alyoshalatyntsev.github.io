Plans for the semester

We'll first learn the main techniques people use to study perverse schobers, then about halfway through will switch to the kind of links with geometric representation theory we'll have seen in the journal club.

Papers we might review:

  • Gaiotto, Moore, Neitzke, Spectral networks, and WKB [1204.4824]
  • Fock, Goncharov, Moduli spaces of local systems and higher Teichmüller theory [math/0311149]
  • Casals, Christ, Categorical Lusztig cycles and weave schobers [2605.22440]
  • Christ, Lampetti, Lagrangian structures on the derived moduli of constructible sheaves [2603.04983]
  • Gaiotto, Moore, Witten, Algebra of the infrared [1408.2673, 2011.00845]
  • Porta, Teyssier, Homotopy theory of Stokes data and The derived moduli of Stokes data [2401.12335, 2504.05360]
  • Kapranov, Schechtman, Shuffle algebras and perverse sheaves [1904.09325]
  • Bridgeland, Stability conditions on triangulated categories [math/0212237]
  • Argüz, Bousseau, attractor flow trees [2102.11200, 2302.02068]
  • Kontsevich, Soibelman, Stability structures, motivic Donaldson–Thomas invariants and cluster transformations, and wall crossing [0811.2435]
  • The KZ equations, and Drinfeld, On quasitriangular quasi-Hopf algebras and a group closely connected with Gal(Q/Q)
  • Cluster algebras and cluster categories, and Hernandez, Leclerc, Cluster algebras and quantum affine algebras [0903.1452]
